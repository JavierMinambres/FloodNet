# Paper traceability

Source of every number and figure in *UAV-Based Flood Mapping for Rescue Missions and Autonomous
Navigation in Challenging Outdoor Environments*, in the frozen state of this repository (tag
`v1.0-paper`).

Notebook cells are given as *section* and *cell index* (0-based, as stored in the `.ipynb`). The three
training notebooks were run once and are not re-run; `floodnet-paper-evaluation.ipynb` reloads their
checkpoints and reproduces their test mIoU before computing anything new. Abbreviations:
**SEG** = `floodnet-segmentation.ipynb`, **DL** = `floodnet-deeplabv3plus.ipynb`,
**YOLO** = `floodnet-yolo11n-seg.ipynb`, **EVAL** = `floodnet-paper-evaluation.ipynb`,
**XLSX** = `floodnet_resultados.xlsx`, **PROV** = `docs/data_provenance.md`.

Values that the paper derives by subtraction or division from other rows (for example "2.3 points
above DeepLabV3+") are marked *derived* and can be checked from the rows they come from.
